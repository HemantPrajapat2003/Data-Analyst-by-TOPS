# Session 3 — Domain Understanding for Analytics

## Task 1: Why is domain knowledge important?
**Selected domain: Flipkart (E-commerce)**

Domain knowledge means understanding how a business works before analyzing its data. In Flipkart, domain knowledge helps an analyst understand sales, customer behavior, product demand, returns, and delivery performance. For example, if sales decrease, an analyst can investigate whether the reason is high prices, low product availability, or delayed delivery. This helps the company make better business decisions.

## Task 2: List 4 KPIs for Flipkart

| KPI | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Revenue | Total money earned from sales. |
| Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) | Average cost of acquiring one new customer. |
| Customer Retention Rate | Percentage of customers who continue buying from the platform. |
| Average Order Value (AOV) | Average amount spent per order. |

**AOV example:** If Flipkart receives ₹1,00,000 from 200 orders:

AOV = Total Revenue ÷ Number of Orders = ₹1,00,000 ÷ 200 = **₹500 per order**.

## Task 3: Define Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) for Paytm

Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) is the average amount a company spends to acquire one new customer.

**Formula:**

CAC = Total Customer Acquisition Cost ÷ Number of New Customers Acquired

**Example:** Suppose Paytm spends ₹50,000 on advertising and promotional campaigns in one month and acquires 1,000 new customers.

CAC = ₹50,000 ÷ 1,000 = **₹50 per new customer**.

This assumes the entire ₹50,000 was spent on acquiring those customers during that period.

## Task 4: Define a new business metric for Swiggy

As a data analyst at Swiggy, I would introduce a metric called **On-Time Delivery Rate** to improve customer satisfaction. It measures the percentage of orders delivered within the promised delivery time.

**Formula:**

On-Time Delivery Rate = (Orders delivered on time ÷ Total completed orders) × 100

For example, if 900 out of 1,000 completed orders arrive on time, the on-time delivery rate is 90%. Swiggy can analyze this metric by restaurant, delivery location, and time of day to identify delays and improve the delivery experience.
